NovaMart Shipping Policy

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days in metro cities and 5 to 8 business days elsewhere in India. Orders above Rs 499 ship free. Orders below Rs 499 have a delivery charge of Rs 49.

Express delivery is available in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune. Express orders placed before 2 PM arrive the next day. Express delivery costs Rs 99.

You can track your order from the "My Orders" page. Tracking updates appear within 24 hours of dispatch.

NovaMart Returns Policy

Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging with all tags attached.

Electronics can be returned within 7 days only if the item is defective or damaged on arrival. Innerwear, cosmetics and perishable groceries cannot be returned.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes quality check. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your NovaMart wallet or a bank account you add in the app.
