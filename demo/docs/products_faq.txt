NovaMart Product FAQ

NovaMart sells electronics, fashion, home and kitchen products, and groceries. Every product page shows the seller name, warranty details and delivery estimate for your pincode.

Warranty claims for electronics are handled by the brand's service centre. NovaMart customer support can share the service centre contact details for your product.

NovaMart Plus is a membership that costs Rs 999 per year. Members get free express delivery, early access to sales and 5 percent extra cashback on the NovaMart credit card.

To change your delivery address, go to "My Orders" and choose "Change address". The address can be changed only before the order is dispatched.

Customer support is available from 8 AM to 10 PM every day through chat, and by email at support@novamart.example.
